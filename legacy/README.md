## About

Computational biologist with experience in transcriptomics, multi-omics, and 3D genomics. I build reproducible pipelines that transform raw sequencing data into biological insights, and develop R and Python packages to streamline downstream analysis. I work with production-grade workflows in Nextflow and Snakemake, containerized with Docker and Singularity, and executed on HPC clusters (SLURM) at Rockefeller University and BigMind Lab.

Research interests:
- Transcriptomics and differential expression analysis
- Multi-omics integration and visualization
- 3D genomics (Hi-C, pileup analysis, loop quantification)
- Foundation models and machine learning for genomics

I am always open to collaborate on interesting projects in academia, biotech, and health-tech.

## Selected Projects

### [plastier](https://github.com/DanielGarbozo/plastier)
Nextflow-based bioinformatics pipeline.  
**Technologies:** Nextflow, Bash, Docker/Singularity, HPC (SLURM)

### [hicpup](https://github.com/DanielGarbozo/hicpup) & [hicpup-nf](https://github.com/DanielGarbozo/hicpup-nf)
Downstream analysis and visualization of Hi-C pileups: fountain cone profiles, P(s) slopes, and loop quantification at paired or singular anchors. `hicpup-nf` is a Nextflow (DSL2) pipeline that converts Juicer `.hic` files to cooler format and runs expected → pileup → cone profiles, wrapping the `hicpup` package.  
**Technologies:** Python, R, Jupyter, Nextflow, cooler, Hi-C

### [OmicsKit](https://github.com/BigMindLab/OmicsKit) (contributor)
R package for streamlining multi-omics analysis and visualization. OmicsKit provides a unified interface for differential expression, pathway enrichment, and integrative multi-omics workflows.  
**Technologies:** R, Bioconductor, multi-omics, RNA-seq, ChIP-seq, ATAC-seq

### [teloscope](https://github.com/vgl-hub/teloscope) (contributor)
Comprehensive telomere annotation tool for genome assemblies and graphs. Teloscope detects and annotates telomeric repeats in long-read assemblies (ONT, PacBio) and supports graph-based genomes.  
**Technologies:** C++, bioinformatics, genome assembly, telomere annotation, ONT, PacBio

### [ercantools](https://github.com/ercanlab/ercantools) (contributor)
General-purpose R tools for bioinformatics and genomics analysis developed in the Ercan Lab. Includes utilities for data processing, visualization, and statistical analysis.  
**Technologies:** R, genomics, transcriptomics, data visualization

### [ClawBio](https://github.com/ClawBio/ClawBio) (contributor)
Bioinformatics-native AI agent skill library. Local-first, reproducible, and open toolkit for agentic genomics and population genetics workflows.  
**Technologies:** Python, AI agents, genomics, reproducibility, population genetics
